hi!

my name is hudson. i live in san francisco. i am 16 and left high school to invent computational mediums that amplify our curiosity and wonder.

i co-founded [mathetic](https://mathetic.com/), a public benefit corporation using cognitive science research to develop new interfaces and operating systems that augment how humans learn, work, and collaborate with ai for scientific discovery.

i also run [papert lab](https://papertlab.org/), a research nonprofit investigating how best to utilize AI to support human learning. we mainly focus on reimagining computing education, though we do not do “edtech.”

i love hearing from earnest people who are relentlessly curious and enjoy exploring rabbit holes. if this is you, please <a href="mailto:hudson@hudsonmp.me" data-callout="hudson [at] hudsonmp [dot] me">reach out</a>—i'll respond to all genuine emails!

also, if you're going through a hard time, shoot me an email and we can chat! this is something i wish i had a couple years ago :)
